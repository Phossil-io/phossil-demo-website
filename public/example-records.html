Product analytics · exported pilot events · May 16

The CSV export contains 18 administrator setup attempts across the five pilot branches. Fifteen completed invitations without assistance; three completed after Support helped them find the control. No permissions-timing error was observed in these 18 attempts. The sample is small, has no comparison group, and excludes the 35 paused branches. It cannot prove the issue is eliminated or measure revenue impact.
